R3-19 [ux, perf] (during the run) Annotations · the thumbnails of the previews take long: after some navigating around, every thumbnail shows "rendering still…" with the busy cursor and the images appear after a delay · wanted: lower the latency. **Item 19 built (below).**
   Where it stands (read from the code, not measured): each thumbnail is its own request, `POST /api/annotation-preview` (`annotations.js`), and the previews are rendered ONE AT A TIME, in the tab (`previewChain`) and again on the server (`serial()` in `annotation-routes.ts`). A request does, in order: the brand and its assets (`prepareBrand`), the geometry and timeline of the take, the overlay for this annotation (`buildRenderAnnotations`: a Chromium is launched for every preview whose overlay PNG is not cached; the launch alone is a second or more), the cache key from all of that, and only then looks for the cached still; an uncached still is then a three-frame ffmpeg slice through the scene's whole render job (the filter chain of a real render) of which one frame is kept. So even a cached thumbnail goes through the queue and the brand preparation, and an uncached one costs a browser launch plus an ffmpeg render, serially for each of the N thumbnails.
   Why it gets worse after navigating around (a likely cause, to confirm): leaving the screen does not cancel anything. The requests of the thumbnails you left are still in the tab's chain and in the server's queue, and the thumbnails of the screen you came back to are queued BEHIND them (and request the same stills again): the queue is full of work nobody is waiting for.
