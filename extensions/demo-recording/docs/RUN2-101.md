# Run 2, step by step: the real-app run on a clean project

Run 2 repeats the first real-app run (keycraft, the same brief) on a **clean, separate working folder**, to find out whether everything built
since (the cost gate, QA as a report, the fix window, pending fixes, timing hints, the new layout) holds up with a real Director and real
data. It is the check that decides what comes next (see [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md)). The commands are for **fish**, one per line.

**Budget in plain words** (estimates, not measured):
- **Time:** about 2 to 3 hours with waiting; you can stop after any stage and come back (the project is just files).
- **Model turns:** discovery, the storyboard, recording (improvised inputs), the voice-over, the annotation designer and every fix turn. The
  panel shows each as a visible Director task.
- **Voice credits:** the only part that costs ElevenLabs. A full synthesis of a script like run 1's (about 1,300 spoken characters) is about
  $0.30 at the 0.22 per 1,000 credits in your config; expect two or three syntheses over the run, so **about $1**. Every synthesis asks
  first and shows its numbers; **Allow** is your confirmation.
- Your **real folder `~/DemoRecordings` is never touched**: the run lives in its own folder.

## 3. Discover, storyboard, record (the stages that did not change much)

Follow [TUTORIAL-101.md](TUTORIAL-101.md) Part B for the mechanics (B2 discover, B3 record). Things to **watch for**, because they are new:

| Where | What should be true | Criterion |
|---|---|---|
| Whenever the Director works | the band says **Director working: <task>** with a step, the strip and the stepper show it, Activity has a Director row | 7 |
| Recording | the Director **asks** to record ("Allow / Not now"); nothing records before you answer | 6 |

## 4. Voice-over, voice and edit

1. Read the voice-over. **Try a timing hint now** (so the later step is quick): in the section of your choice click **+100** a few times under
   an anchor's picture (the narration shows `[@mark +300ms]`), **Save new version**. **See:** the compare line shows exactly that one change.
2. **Approve & use**. The Director asks to synthesize ("The Director asks to synthesize voice-over vN (about N characters)"). **Read the
   numbers, then Allow.** **See:** progress; a draft render in **Render**.
3. Now make the hint a real test of criterion 5: change the hint (another +100 or −100), **Save new version**, approve it, **Generate voice &
   render**. **See in the cost dialog: 0 characters billed** (the words did not change). If it says anything else, **Cancel** and tell me.

## 5. Annotations and the final

Annotations as in the tutorial (B6). Then **Render → New render… → Final → Render now**. When the final is done:

1. **Re-run QA** if no report is there (it is a check of the finished video: it renders nothing and costs nothing; the status bar says
   **Quality check**).
2. **Criterion 1, the report:** the Quality check shows hard stops (if any), **Needs your eyes**, **Worth a look** and one collapsed line for
   held frames. **Approve final** must be possible with findings in it. Note anything that stops you that is not a broken file or a leaking
   redaction.
3. **Criterion 2, numbers you can trust:** for every timing finding both numbers are said (measured, and what the edit planned). Open **All
   checks…**: the coverage line says at how many anchors sync was measured. Compare scene by scene with run 1 (the old numbers are at the
   end of this page).
4. **The new layout on real data:** with the findings list long, scroll it. The **preview should stay pinned** under the header and scroll
   away only after the last finding; the video is about two fifths wide. Try 1400 px and 900 px.
5. **Criterion 4, decisions are remembered:** on a finding that is fine, click **Ignore…**, choose a scope and give a reason. Re-run QA:
   it is under **Ignored by you**, not asked again. Try the scope "in the whole project" on a kind of finding you never want to hear
   about, and check it holds after the next final.

## 6. The fix loop (criterion 3: fixing is not hidden)

1. On the final's findings click **Fix** on a few (the toolbar flags many). **Suggested fixes…** opens the **window**. Add context if you
   like, **Ask the Director**. While it works press **Esc**: the window closes, the Director keeps going, the line under the findings says
   "waiting"; reopen it with **Open suggested fixes**.
2. **Read the answer.** **Be honest here, this is the part that has never run with a real model:** is it bullets? Does it name the stage and
   give the steps to a new render? Is it one proposal per stage? Did it refuse or shorten itself because of the limits (an error message
   from the tool is a finding)? Does it make sense for the real cause?
3. **Accept** one or two, **Edit the wording** on one, add a note, **Ask the Director to rethink** once with more context.
4. **Apply**. **See:** exactly **one new draft** at the earliest stage, linked ("fixes from render vN"); **no render and no voice synthesis
   started**; later fixes are **pending fixes** shown on their stage's screen. Read the draft: did it do only what you accepted?
5. Approve the draft, let the stage writers take the pending fixes into account, and **settle** them (the Director says which it applied or
   dropped; **Settled** on the stage screen). Render again.

(This exact check was run on a copy of your run-1 final v8 while writing this guide: it blocked.) Whisper is not needed for it.
